# Virtara business audit — 2026-11-11

Scope: website, pricing, onboarding, emails, top 5 competitors.
Output: the 10 changes most likely to make money, ranked, each with evidence.

Evidence labels:
- **[code]** read in this repo (file:line)
- **[live]** observed on production today (curl or headless browser)
- **[web]** read on a competitor's public page (URL given); fetched through a summarising tool, so re-check exact wording before quoting it publicly
- **[mail]** seen in the connected Gmail (subject lines and labels only)
- **[inferred]** my reasoning, not directly observed

## Part 1 — Ranked list

### 1. Make the site readable by Google (it currently tells Google every page is the home page)

- [live] Every URL returns the same 3,968-byte HTML with an empty `<div id="root">`. No copy, prices or links without JavaScript.
- [live] `index.html` hard-codes `<link rel="canonical" href="https://virtara.co.za/">` and a home-page description. After JavaScript runs, every page carries **two** canonicals and two descriptions; the first one points at the home page. Checked on `/`, `/services`, `/concierge`, `/resources/audit`.
- [live] A made-up URL returns HTTP 200, so there are no real 404s.
- [web] A `site:virtara.co.za` search and three name searches returned nothing from the domain. The search tool is not Google South Africa, so confirm in Search Console.
- [web] All five competitors serve their copy and prices as plain HTML.
- Change: delete the static canonical and description from `index.html` (minutes), then prerender the public routes at build time. Remove `/brand` from the sitemap (`vite.config.js`).
- Why first: no other change matters much if buyers searching "web design Johannesburg" can never land here.

### 2. Turn analytics on and count leads

- [live] The production bundle contains no GA measurement ID and no Google tag code; `window.gtag` is undefined on every page.
- [code] `src/lib/analytics.js:1-6` returns early when `VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` is missing; `.env` holds only Firebase keys.
- [code] No form fires a conversion event. The cookie banner stores a choice but controls nothing (`src/components/CookieConsent.jsx:16-23`).
- Change: set the GA ID in Vercel, fire one event per form submit, and tie loading to the consent choice.
- Why second: it earns nothing itself, but without it you cannot tell which of the other nine worked. There is currently no record of how many people visit or enquire.

### 3. Collapse the "audit" offer into one page with a real booking

- [code] "Book a strategy audit" / "Book an audit" leads to three different places: a 3-question quiz (`Home.jsx:184`), a generic contact form (`menus.js:109`), and an 8-field project brief (`PlanCard.jsx:14-15`). The actual booking page, `/resources/audit`, is only reachable from the Resources menu.
- [code] The quiz throws its answers away: the result button links to `/start-your-project` with nothing carried over and no email captured (`Home.jsx:131`).
- [code] The quiz's first question offers "R350k–R900k" as the lowest monthly digital revenue target (`Home.jsx:21`), while the entry product is a R12,500 site. Most starter buyers will not see themselves in any option. [inferred]
- [code] The booking page writes to Firestore only (`firebase.js:92-116`). Nothing in this repo notifies you or emails the visitor, yet the thank-you page says "You'll receive a confirmation email shortly" (`AuditThankYou.jsx:9`). A notifier may exist in the separate CRM project; I could not see one.
- [code] The audit is free on the site but "R299, was R499" on the checklist thank-you page (`HealthThankYou.jsx:15-16`), which links to the same free form.
- Change: one audit page, one price (or free), one calendar. Cal.com is already wired into Concierge. Point every audit button at it.

### 4. Get public reviews and named client proof

- [web] Four of five competitors display a rating: Web Partner 4.8 (450+), Lamus 4.8 (308), Juicy Designs 4.9 (214), Online By Digital 5.0 (71). These are self-reported on their sites.
- [web] Juicy Designs charges R12,000 / R28,000 for the same two tiers Virtara sells at R12,500 / R28,000, and backs them with those 214 reviews and a "+312% qualified leads" case study.
- [web] No Google Business Profile, Clutch, Sortlist, GoodFirms or Hellopeter listing for Virtara turned up in search.
- [code] The site shows five client names as plain text and five KPI figures that `PRODUCT.md` marks as unconfirmed. There are no testimonials; the ones in `src/constants/index.js:85-116` are stock photos with invented names and are rightly unused.
- Change: create the Google Business Profile for 16 Hume Road, ask the five clients plus KB Legal for a review each, and replace the KPI tiles with one confirmed number and one named quote per client.

### 5. Answer leads faster, on the channel buyers use

- [live] No WhatsApp link anywhere on the site. The phone number appears only in the footer and contact page.
- [web] Four of five competitors have click-to-chat WhatsApp; two put a phone number in the header. Juicy promises a reply "within four working hours", Lamus "within one business day".
- [code] The three package forms are titled "Submit & book a call" but have no phone field; `phone` is in the state and never rendered (`WebDevForm.jsx:87-103`, `Starter.jsx:14`).
- [code] Form emails go to info@ with no reply-to and no copy to the visitor (`virtara-backend/index.js:63-68`). Leads are stored nowhere else, so a missed email is a lost lead.
- [mail] Concierge lead alerts from leads@virtara.co.za are being filed by Gmail under Promotions and Updates; one waitlist alert sat unread in Promotions.
- [live] I sent one labelled test through `/api/send-email`; the endpoint returned 200. I could not see the info@ inbox to confirm arrival.
- Change: WhatsApp button and phone in the header, a phone field on every form, reply-to set to the lead, an instant auto-reply, and each lead saved to the database as well as emailed.

### 6. Let people buy Concierge now

- [live] `concierge.virtara.co.za` has a login page only. `/signup`, `/sign-up` and `/pricing` return 404. The marketing page offers a waitlist.
- [code] Recent commits say bookings, quote forms and the weekly email are live, so the product exists; it just cannot be bought.
- [web] Every comparable tool can be started today: Tidio (free plan, 7-day trial, no card), Crisp (14 days, no card), Chatling (free plan), Chatbotz, a South African rival (7 days, no card, R999 for 1,000 messages).
- [web] Concierge is good value in the middle: Growth is R1.30 per chat against roughly R10.70 for Tidio's AI add-on and R3.48–R8.26 for Crisp.
- [code] Pro is the weak tier: R1,200 more than Growth for 1,000 extra chats and "priority support" only (`Concierge.jsx:36-42`). Two Growth plans cost R2,598.
- [web] Competitors offer annual discounts (10–40%) and WhatsApp; Concierge has neither.
- Change: replace the waitlist with "we set it up for you, free for 14 days", taken through the existing contact route until self-serve sign-up exists. Give Pro something real (extra sites, WhatsApp handoff, no branding) or drop it.

### 7. Show one set of prices everywhere

- [code] `/services` sells Launch R12,500 / Growth R28,000 / Commerce from R55,000 (`Services.jsx:50-78`). The Pricing menu links to "Starter / Professional / Enterprise" pages that show no price and different contents: 4 pages against "up to 5", and e-commerce inside Professional (`WebDevForm.jsx:7-25`).
- [code] The project brief uses a third scheme: "Starter (R8,000 – R25,000)" (`StartaProject.jsx:157-162`).
- [code] Maintenance is R1,000 / R2,500 / R5,000 on its own page (`MaintenanceSupport.jsx:22-45`) and R1,200 / R2,500 / R4,500 as "care plans" on `/services`.
- [code] Every plan button on `/services` opens the same blank brief; the chosen plan is not passed along.
- [web] Two competitors have the same fault (Juicy lists e-commerce at both R25,000 and R85,000; Web Partner prices one tier two ways), so consistency is an easy way to look more trustworthy than them.
- Change: one price table in one file, used by every page; plan buttons pre-select the plan.

### 8. Add a low-risk way to start, and print delivery times

- [web] Lamus builds a free homepage preview before any payment and includes a domain and a year's hosting. WebX and Web Partner spread the cost monthly (from R195 and R299 a month). Juicy prints delivery time on each tier (2–3, 4–6, 8–16 weeks).
- [code] Virtara states no delivery time, deposit or payment terms on any package. The terms page says only that payment terms "vary" (`TermsOfService.jsx`, section 4).
- [web] Virtara's SEO setup fees (R3,500–R8,500) are the highest found; Juicy advertises none.
- Change: print weeks-to-launch and the deposit split on each build tier, and offer the Launch Site as a deposit plus monthly instalments. Consider folding the SEO setup fee into the first three months.

### 9. Sell recurring plans to the clients you already have

- [mail] Hosting invoices are being sent by hand each month to at least one client, and a second client was told in October that hosting would now be charged. I read subject lines and snippets only, not amounts.
- [mail] KB Legal already has a Concierge assistant running on its site (a test lead came through it on 9 November).
- [code] Care plans start at R1,000 a month and Concierge at R599, but neither page mentions existing clients.
- [inferred] Five to six past clients moved onto a care plan plus Concierge is a faster route to monthly income than any new-visitor change, because they already trust you. This rests on thin evidence; you know the client relationships and I do not.
- Change: write to each past client with a single bundled monthly offer (hosting, care, Concierge) and a debit order.

### 10. Follow up the checklist download, and revive the blog

- [code] The checklist form stores the email in Firestore and adds it to a Sender list tagged `website-health-check` (`virtara-backend/index.js:23-34`). Any emails after that depend on an automation inside Sender that I could not see.
- [code] A returning visitor whose email is already stored gets "We couldn't send the checklist just now" (`HealthCheck.jsx` with `firebase.js:42-47`).
- [live] The checklist PDF itself is served correctly (204 KB).
- [live] The blog has 3 posts, the newest dated 23 December 2024, none aimed at a South African search ("Tech Trends 2024…").
- Change: a three-email sequence after the download ending in the audit offer; let repeat emails through; publish pages that match what buyers search, such as website cost in South Africa and web design in Johannesburg. This depends on item 1.
