Virtara Style Guide & Branding

Version 1.0 | Generated from current implementation | Date: 2026-02-26

1. Brand Core

Brand personality: Confident, technical, modern, outcome-focused.

Visual direction: Dark premium interface with electric cyan/blue accents, soft glass effects, and high-contrast typography.

Positioning cue: “Digital assets that outperform” (performance + measurable growth).

2. Logo & Identity

  • Use the existing brand logo assets from /public/web_logo_bg.svg and derived image assets.
  • Maintain clear space around logo (minimum 1x logo mark height on all sides).
  • Preferred placement: top-left in navigation and neutral/dark backgrounds.
  • Avoid recoloring outside approved palette or placing logo on noisy backgrounds without contrast treatment.

5. Layout, Spacing & Shape

  • Default layout: dark full-viewport sections with centered content containers.
  • Radii language: rounded cards and pills (rounded-full, rounded-[26px], rounded-[32px]).
  • Section rhythm: generous vertical spacing (py-20 to py-24 patterns).
  • Glass surfaces: translucent whites with subtle borders and backdrop blur.

6. Component Styling Patterns

Buttons

  • Primary CTA: pill shape, cyan-blue gradient, strong shadow, slight hover brightening.
  • Secondary: outlined, low-opacity backgrounds, preserved high contrast.

Cards

  • Use translucent backgrounds (white/5 to white/10) with soft borders.
  • Use gradient border treatment for emphasis cards and pricing highlights.

Forms

  • Inputs on dark surfaces with subtle borders and cyan focus rings.
  • Error/success feedback should remain explicit and near the field or action.

7. Motion & Interaction

  • Primary motion library: Framer Motion.
  • Typical transitions: fade + slight translate (0.5s to 0.8s) for staged reveals.
  • Hover/tap micro-interactions on buttons/cards (scale and opacity changes).
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences where possible.

8. Imagery & Visual Effects

  • Backgrounds use radial/linear gradients for depth (not flat fills).
  • Use blur glows and layered gradients to create a premium technical atmosphere.
  • Project imagery should be high-contrast and framed in dark overlays for legibility.

9. Voice & Messaging Guidelines

  • Tone: direct, strategic, results-first.
  • Copy style: concise, benefit-led, commercial clarity over buzzwords.
  • CTA language: action-oriented (“Start Your Project”, “Book Audit”, “Claim Now”).

10. Accessibility & Quality Baselines

  • Maintain sufficient color contrast for all body text against dark backgrounds.
  • Ensure keyboard focus states are visible on buttons/links/inputs.
  • Use descriptive alt text and semantic heading hierarchy (single H1 per page).
  • Preserve responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
